# Week-one selling plan

## Goal
Close the first pilot client next week.

## Offer
Sell installation + monthly fee.

## Fast script
"I built an independent business operating system that helps companies respond faster, quote better, control tasks, track basic cashflow and improve pricing decisions. I can install a pilot version for your business and adapt it to your workflow."

## Outreach target
- 20 local businesses or business owners you can contact directly
- 5 warm contacts first
- 5 demos goal
- 1 paid pilot goal

## Daily rhythm
- Morning: send outreach
- Midday: run demos
- Afternoon: send follow-ups and proposal
- Evening: refine objections and next-day list

## Pilot pricing
- Setup: charge once
- Monthly: keep it simple and recurring

## Close trigger
Offer a 7-day pilot setup window and a simple onboarding form.
