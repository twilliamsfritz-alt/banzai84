# Vantis One — Section Guide

## Overview
High-level summary of what the application does and a readiness checklist.

## Sales
Contains the conversation inbox, the active conversation panel, the human reply composer, the webchat intake form and the quote builder.

## Admin
Contains internal tasks, ownership, status, impact and due labels.

## Finance
Contains the ledger of income and expenses. Quote creation can create pending income entries here.

## Pricing
Lets you select a product and run a pricing recommendation based on cost, competitor price and demand score.

## Knowledge
Contains business rules and reusable templates.

## Backend
Explains what the backend is, what layers exist and which API routes power the app.

## Responses
Lets you define how the assistant should sound so replies are more human and less robotic.

## Backup
Use the backup button in the top bar to download a zipped copy of the local database.
